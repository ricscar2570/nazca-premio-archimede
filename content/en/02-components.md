# PART II — PHYSICAL COMPONENTS

> [!WARNING]
> SPATIAL REDESIGN v8: Terrain tiles are NO LONGER on the board. Cultivated fields are off the table, on the playing surface, along each clan's chosen slope. The pampa is now a pure space: geoglyphs, water sources, huarango trees, pegs and cords.

## 2.1  The board (pampa)

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Model | Quercetti Pixel Art 4 — 4 assembled panels |

## 2.3  Pegs — no limit

> [!NOTE]
> DESIGN DECISION v8: No constraint on the number of pegs for any clan. The cord (physical length of the reel ~1 metre) is the true scarce resource — not the pegs.

| Type | Colour | Clan/Use |
|------|--------|----------|
| Clan pegs | Red / Blue / Green / White-Grey | One colour per clan. Unlimited. |
| Puquio pegs | Terracotta / Brown | One per source where a puquio is built. Unlimited. Shared. |
| Orca neutral pegs | Black or Transparent | Orca-exclusive. Mark blocked holes. Unlimited. |

## 2.4  Field tiles (5 per player)

Field tiles are not on the board. Each player has 5 double-sided tiles ON THE TABLE, along their chosen slope. Soil side → irrigated side.

| Type | Qty/player | Ideal crop | Bonus |
|------|-----------|------------|-------|
| Alluvial (ochre) | 2 | Maize / Squash | Maize and squash mature in 1 round instead of 2 |
| Clay (grey-brown) | 2 | Beans | Free sowing (0 water) instead of 1 water |
| Sandy (beige) | 1 | Cotton | Cotton matures in 1 round instead of 2 |

## 2.5  Cards

| Type | Quantity | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Monumental Geoglyph Cards | 24 (6 per clan) | The secret path the clan must trace between sources. One per player, face-down. |
| Minor Geoglyph Cards | 36 | Secondary objectives. Simpler paths. |

| El Niño Cards | 3 | Secret threshold 4, 5 or 6. Drawn face-down at game start. |
| Clan Cards | 4 | Special power summary. Always visible in front of the player. |
