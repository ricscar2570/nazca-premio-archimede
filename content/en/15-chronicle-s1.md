## 15.10  Session 1 post-mortem — patch-by-patch analysis

### Patch P1 — El Niño (thresholds 4/5/6, felling +1)
CONFIRMED structurally (rare felling = manageable El Niño). One felling in the whole game (Beppe, R4). El Niño did not trigger (1/5). [NOTE: Session 1 used P1v1 with +1 water. Current rule P1v3 uses +2 water + free 1×/game action — more fellings and more El Niño tension expected.]

### Patch P2 — Squash 2 rounds
**DECISIVE.** Without this patch Chiara would have scored 36 pt instead of 40 — Diego would have won. Squash is now playable within 6 rounds. Patch confirmed.

### Patch P3 — Spider (2+ ADJ + odd round)
**PARTIALLY CONFIRMED.** Bonus activated only once (R5). Anna finishes at 21 pt — no longer dominant. The double condition was too restrictive in physical play. Already implemented as PATCH P3v2: reduced to 1 peg ADJ + odd round (current rule).

### Patch P4 — Orca directional block
**MIXED RESULT.** Directional block no longer penalises the Orca (no self-block). Beppe placed 5 blocks, one poorly placed. Structural problem confirmed: Orca finishes with 0 harvests, 0 agricultural points. 19 on-path nodes + Art rule = 21 pt.

### Patch P5 — Art Bonus +2, anti-leader rule
**FULL SUCCESS.** The anti-leader rule changed the final outcome: Diego (leader) loses the Art Bonus, Beppe receives +2 pt tying with Anna. Final result 40-40-21-21 is more interesting than a clear win. Patch confirmed.

### Hummingbird Mobility — canonical verification
**WORKS.** Chiara uses the power every round with precision: +1 useful node/round × 6 rounds = 6 nodes gained. Completion 73% (Chiara's maximum in any session). Mobility is silently powerful: it makes no noise during the game but weighs heavily in the final calculation.

### Condor Information every round — canonical verification
**EFFECTIVE.** The surgical block on C-11 cost Anna 2 extra actions — a concrete measurable loss. Diego had complete information on all clans by Round 5. Limit: information is only valuable when there is physical space to act on it.
