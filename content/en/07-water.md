# PART VII — THE WATER SYSTEM

> [!WARNING]
> REDESIGN v8 — two geographic planes: Water originates on the pampa (underground sources), is channelled through the peg-cord network to the board edge (puquio + chain), and flows into the valley (off the board) where it irrigates the fields. There is no longer irrigation on the board.

## 7.1  The three states of water

> [!NOTE]
> All water states are physically represented by blue tokens in concrete positions. There is no "in transit" state in which a token needs to be physically balanced on the cord — that would be ergonomically disastrous and mechanically unnecessary. The cord is a visual indicator of the path; the token moves only between the puquio node and the personal reserve.

| State | Physical position | How it changes |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| 🔵 BLOCKED | Blue token on the puquio peg (source node) — visible to all | The chain reaches the slope edge → the token moves to the personal reserve |
| 🟢 AVAILABLE | Personal reserve behind the clan screen | Spendable immediately: 1 water (maize/beans/squash) · 2 water (cotton) · 0 on clay (beans) |
| ⚡ PRODUCED | Generated from the central supply at end of round — goes directly to Available | +1 per puquio with chain connected to the edge |

**Complete flow:** place the puquio → put a token on the terracotta peg (Blocked). Trace toward the edge. When the chain touches the edge → move the token to your reserve (Available). Each end of round: add 1 token from the central supply for each puquio still connected (Produced → Available).

## 7.2  The chain — two functions in one cord

The cord of each clan does two things simultaneously:

- **Inner part (base → sources):** draws the geoglyph. Follows the path on the secret card.
- **Outer part (puquio → edge):** builds the puquio-aqueduct. Carries water from the subsoil toward the valley.

They are not two different cords — it is the same one. At the Revelation, when everyone stands up, both functions are seen simultaneously.

## 7.3  Sources — visible to all, map is asymmetric

The 12 sources (S1–S12) are visible to all on the overlay. But hydrological knowledge is asymmetric: each clan only knows the sources indicated on their own geoglyph card.

**Puquio constraint (structural rule):** you may only build a puquio on sources from your geoglyph card. Physically reaching another player's source does not give you the right to dig there — you don't have the map of that underground flow.

Reaching one of your card's sources does not oblige you to build the puquio. Some sources may not justify the excavation — too far from the edge, too much cord consumed.

## 7.4  Automatic production at end of round

At the end of each round: for each puquio with an unbroken chain to the edge, the player receives 1 water token in their personal reserve.

**Network efficiency:** having 3 puquios connected to the edge = +3 water/round automatic, every round. Initial construction is slow but the return is cumulative and permanent.

## 7.5  Continuity bonus

If the chain has 5+ consecutive nodes all on the geoglyph card path (on-path), the player gains +1 water at the start of each of their turns. The bonus lapses if the path is broken by an opponent's block.
