# PART XII — FINAL SCORING

## 12.1  Score table

| Category | Value | Notes |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Mature harvests (maize) | 3 pt per tile | From the valley scoring area |
| Mature harvests (beans) | 2 pt per tile | + returns 1 water at time of harvest |
| Mature harvests (squash) | 4 pt per tile | Matures in 2 rounds (1 round on alluvial) |
| Mature harvests (cotton) | 2 pt per tile | + returns 1 water on sandy |
| Three Sisters bonus | +5 pt per triad | ≥1 maize + ≥1 beans + ≥1 squash in scoring area — no adjacency |
| Farming bonus (≥2 fields) | +3 pt | If you have harvested from at least 2 different fields |
| On-path nodes (+1 pt/node) | 1 pt per node | Clan pegs on the geoglyph card path |
| Continuity bonus | +1 water/turn | NOT points — it is bonus water. 5+ consecutive on-path nodes → +1 water at start of turn. |
| Completion bonus >72% | +5 pt | Percentage: (on-path nodes) / (total path nodes) |
| Completion penalty <50% | −3 pt | |
| Geometric objectives | +3 pt per objective | First player to complete the shape |
| Art Bonus | +2 pt | Blind vote — not to the harvest leader |
| Individual huarango penalty | −1 pt per felling | From the player who felled |
| El Niño collective penalty | −3 pt to all | If total fellings ≥ threshold |
| Survival bonus (0 fellings) | +3 pt to all | If no huarango was felled |

## 12.2  Geoglyph completion calculation

Completion % = (nodes with clan peg matching the card path) ÷ (total nodes on the card path) × 100. Off-path nodes do not count.

## 12.3  Tiebreakers

- First: more harvest tiles in personal scoring area
- Second: higher geoglyph completion percentage
- Third: tied players both win
