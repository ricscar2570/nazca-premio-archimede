# PART XIII — BALANCE AND PARAMETERS

## 13.1  Simulation status

The spatial redesign v8 has not yet been simulated (300k games). The parameters reported are from simulation v3 (Game Bible v6) — valid as a baseline but requiring re-validation with the new pampa/valley system.

> [!WARNING]
> ⚠ CRITICAL RISK v8 — WATER INFLATION: In the previous system Irrigation cost 2 water — a large dissipator that kept the reserve scarce. In the v8 redesign irrigation is free and sowing costs only 1 water (2 for cotton). Puquios automatically produce +1 water/round each. With 2–3 active puquios from Round 3–4, a player accumulates 2–3 water/round with no equivalent outlets. PARAMETER TO MONITOR #1 in the solo playthrough: track each clan's water reserve round by round. If it regularly exceeds 8–10 tokens mid-game, the water mechanic is inflated. Candidate solutions: (a) reduce automatic production to +1 every 2 rounds, (b) increase cotton sowing cost to 3, (c) introduce a water cost for branching.

## 13.3  Balance targets

- Each clan: ~25% win rate (tolerance ±8%)
- El Niño: 35–50% activation at 4 players
- Three Sisters: at least 1 triad in 40–60% of games
- Art: to leader <60% of games
- Median winner score: 15–22 pt
- Duration: 55 ± 15 minutes at 4 players (6 rounds)
- No player eliminated before Round 5

## 13.4  Acetate silhouettes — Geoglyph recognisability

To improve the Revelation moment: 4 transparent acetate sheets with the stylised silhouette of each animal. At the Revelation they are overlaid on the board quadrants. The brain immediately recognises how well the physical cord fits the archetypal silhouette.

Cost: print 4 sheets on transparency film. No simulation needed — a pure physical improvement. To build for Prototype Zero.
