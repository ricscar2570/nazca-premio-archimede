# PART XVI — PLAYTEST SHEETS

## 16.1  Post-game guide questions

Ask each participant after each playtest session:

| Area | Question |
|------|----------|
| Clan balance | Did your clan feel fair? Which clan won most often? |
| Powers | Did you use your clan's special power? Was it decisive? |
| Water | Is the water flow system clear? Did you ever lose track of blocked water? |
| El Niño | Did the El Niño tension feel real? Did you fell a Huarango? Why or why not? |
| Three Sisters | Did you try for Three Sisters? Did you manage it? |
| Revelation | Did the Revelation moment move you? Was the geoglyph recognisable? |
| Duration | Did the game feel too long, too short, or just right? |
| Rules | Was there anything unclear or that felt unfair? |
| Replayability | Would you play again immediately? Which clans haven't you played yet? |

## 16.3  The golden rule of playtesting

> [!NOTE]
> After every game you have 24 hours to modify the prototype. Don't accumulate feedback: fix immediately, play again, fix again. Have three people who have never played read the rulebook and ask them to explain it to you. If they make a mistake, the problem is in the rulebook — not the reader.
