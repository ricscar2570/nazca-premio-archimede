# PART IV — TURN STRUCTURE

## 4.1  General flow

The game lasts 6 fixed rounds (8-round option for extended sessions). Each round has three phases in order:

| Phase | Who acts | What happens |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| 1. MATURATION | Automatic — all | All crops in valley fields advance 1 step toward maturity. |
| 2. ACTIONS | Each player in turn | Each player takes 3 actions + optional free huarango felling. At the start of their turn, a player receives +1 water if the continuity bonus is active (5+ consecutive on-path nodes). |
| 3. WATER PRODUCTION | Automatic — all | Each puquio with a chain connected to the board edge produces 1 water for its owner. |

## 4.2  The 3 actions per turn

Each player takes exactly 3 actions per turn, freely chosen from the 7 available (Part V, actions 5.1–5.7). Actions may be repeated. The special action 5.8 (Fell a Huarango) is free and outside the count. The Condor uses the spy power outside the count.

## 4.3  End-game triggers

- 6 rounds completed (main timer)
- A player has exhausted their cord reel (can no longer trace or branch)
- All players pass consecutively without taking any action
