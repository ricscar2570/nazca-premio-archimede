# PART VI — THE FOUR ASYMMETRIC CLANS

## 6.1  SPIDER (Red) ✦ The Web

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Pegs | Unlimited (red) |
| Power — THE WEB [P3v2] | On odd rounds (1, 3, 5): if you plant a peg adjacent to 1+ opponent peg, gain +1 water bonus. On even rounds the power does not activate. |
| Style | Aggressive. Thrives on density, close to opponents. Incentive to expand toward the centre where other clans pass. |
| Strength | High water economy in dense central rounds · Passive disruption |
| Weakness | Penalised if opponents stay near edges · Depends on opponents' topology |
:::

## 6.2  ORCA (Blue) ◆ The Water Block

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Pegs | Unlimited (blue) + unlimited neutrals (black/transparent) |
| Power — WATER BLOCK [P4v2] | (A) When planting a blue peg, choose direction E-W or N-S: plant 2 neutral pegs in adjacent holes in that direction (physically blocked). (B) When planting a blue peg adjacent to an existing own neutral peg: +1 immediate water. |
| Power — CANALS [PROP1 rev. v8] | When the Orca's chain reaches the slope edge by passing through a node adjacent to one or more own neutral pegs, gain −1 water on the next SOW for each neutral ADJ to the border node touched (minimum 0). The bonus is measured entirely on the board — it does not depend on the physical position of field tiles off the board. |
| Style | Territorial control with water income. Place neutrals near your slope's border nodes to accumulate sowing discounts. |
| Strength | Self-executing physical block · Water bonus for neutral network · Discounted sowing |
| Weakness | Block direction is permanent — a mistake costs space · Requires advanced planning |
:::

> [!NOTE]
> CANAL EXAMPLE: The Orca has placed neutrals at T-25 and T-26 (near the East edge). In Round 4 its chain reaches the East edge by planting a blue peg at T-27 (border node). T-27 is adjacent to T-26 (neutral) → gains −1 water on the next sow. If T-27 were adjacent to both T-26 and S-27 (two neutrals), it would gain −2 water. The calculation happens entirely on the board.

## 6.3  HUMMINGBIRD (Green) ★ Mobility

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Pegs | Unlimited (green) |
| Power — MOBILITY+ [P6] | Once per round, outside the 3-action count: move one of your placed pegs to an adjacent empty node. The cord reattaches. If the destination node is a source that appears on your own geoglyph card: +1 immediate water. (Any source is not enough — it must be a source on your own path.) |
| Style | Flexible. Corrects wrong paths, evades blocks, systematically targets sources with the move. The only clan that modifies the network after laying it. |
| Strength | Resistance to blocks · Higher geoglyph completion at game end |
| Weakness | Value decreases in late rounds when nearby sources are already occupied |
:::

## 6.4  CONDOR (White/Grey) ▲ Information

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Pegs | Unlimited (white/grey) |
| Power — INFORMATION | Once per round, outside the 3-action count: choose an opponent and look at their geoglyph card for 3 seconds. May choose different opponents each round. With 4 players (3 opponents), the Condor has seen all cards by Round 3; with 2 players, 2 rounds suffice. |
| Style | Deductive. Knows where opponents are going, plans surgical blocks or optimises own path toward uncontested sources. |
| Strength | Growing information · Precise blocks · Knows opponents' optimal slope |
| Weakness | No direct water bonus · Value depends on ability to act on information |
:::
