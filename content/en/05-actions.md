# PART V — THE 7 ACTIONS + 1 SPECIAL

## 5.1  TRACE

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Cost | 0 water — no peg limit |
| Effect | Plant 1 clan peg and stretch the cord |
:::

Plant a peg at a node adjacent to one already occupied by your peg (or your base node). Stretch the cord. No limit on the number of pegs.

**Cord budget — the true scarce resource:** the reel holds about 1 metre of cord. When it runs out you can no longer trace or branch. Managing the cord is a central strategic decision.

**Territorial blocking:** you cannot plant at a node already occupied by an opponent's peg, another player's puquio, or an Orca neutral peg.

**Note on the cord:** the same cord draws the geoglyph in the inner area of the board AND builds the canal toward the edge in the outer area. They are the same action — trace — but with two overlapping narrative meanings.

## 5.2  PUQUIO ▲ [NEW ACTION]

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Cost | 1 action — 0 water |
| Effect | Plant a terracotta peg on the source reached by your chain |
| FUNDAMENTAL CONSTRAINT | The puquio may only be placed on sources that appear on your secret geoglyph card |
:::

When your chain has reached a source node (S1–S12) shown on your geoglyph card, you may spend 1 action to place a puquio peg (terracotta, from the shared pouch) there.

> [!WARNING]
> KEY RULE — Only your card's sources: The 12 sources on the board are visible to all, but not all belong to your clan. The geoglyph is your clan's hydrological map — the partial, secret knowledge of underground flows that ONLY your clan possesses. You may only build a puquio where your card says reachable water exists.

> [!NOTE]
> PUQUIO EFFECT: (1) The node generates 1 blocked water token on the terracotta peg. (2) The puquio is visible to all. (3) When the chain touches your slope's edge, the token moves to your reserve. (4) Automatic production: +1 water/round while connected to the edge.

### Why the geoglyph is not optional — four incentive levels

| Mechanic | Incentive to follow the geoglyph | Penalty for deviating |
|----------|----------------------------------|----------------------|
| VP per on-path node | +1 pt per peg on the card's path | 0 pt off-path — wasted actions and cord |
| Completion thresholds | +5 pt if >72% of geoglyph drawn | −3 pt if <50% — devastating fixed penalty |
| Continuity bonus | +1 water/turn with 5+ consecutive on-path pegs | No water income — weak economy all game |
| Art Bonus | Votes if the geoglyph is recognisable — +2 pt | A straight line looks like no animal — 0 votes |

## 5.3  IRRIGATE TO VALLEY ▲ [MODIFIED]

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Cost | 1 action — 0 water |
| Effect | Flip 1 field tile off the board to the irrigated side |
:::

You may only take this action if your chain has reached the board edge on your chosen slope.

> [!NOTE]
> PHYSICAL DEFINITION OF "EDGE": "Touching the edge" means having a clan peg planted in the outermost row of grid nodes on your slope's side — it is not enough for the cord to hang over the edge. Border nodes by slope: **North = row 1 · South = row 27 · East = column T (20th) · West = column A (1st).** A peg in that border node activates valley irrigation.

## 5.4  SOW

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Cost | 1 water for maize, beans, squash · 2 water for cotton · 0 water on clay (beans) |
| Effect | Place a crop tile on an irrigated valley field |
:::

| Crop | Maturation | Points | Ideal terrain | Terrain bonus |
|------|-----------|--------|---------------|---------------|
| Maize | 2 rounds · 1 on alluvial | 3 pt | Alluvial | Matures in 1 round on alluvial |
| Beans | 1 round | 2 pt | Clay | Free sowing on clay · returns 1 water at harvest |
| Squash | 2 rounds · 1 on alluvial | 4 pt | Alluvial | Matures in 1 round on alluvial — 2 rounds on any other terrain |
| Cotton | 2 rounds · 1 on sandy | 2 pt | Sandy | Sowing: 2 water · matures in 1 round on sandy · returns 1 water at harvest |

## 5.5  HARVEST

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Cost | 0 |
| Effect | Take the points of a mature crop · beans and cotton on sandy also return 1 water |
:::

If a crop has reached the required number of rounds, it is mature. Take the tile and add it to your scoring area. The field remains irrigated. Beans return 1 water; cotton on sandy returns 1 water.

## 5.6  BRANCH

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Cost | 0 |
| Effect | Restart the cord from an already-placed peg |
:::

Return to one of your existing pegs and pull the cord in a new direction toward a free adjacent node. The cord forks. Branching uses cord — plan your reel budget.

## 5.7  PASS

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Cost | 0 |
| Effect | Forfeit remaining actions for the turn |
:::

You may pass at any point in your turn, forfeiting actions not yet taken. You may pass before taking any action. If all players pass consecutively without taking any action, the game ends.

## 5.8  FELL A HUARANGO ★ [FREE ACTION 1×/GAME]

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Type | FREE action — outside the 3-action count. Additional fellings beyond the first cost 1 normal action. |
| Frequency | 1 time per game per player is free — choose the best round. Further fellings are possible but cost an action. |
| Effect | +2 immediate water + flip 1 valley field tile for free (without spending the Irrigate action) |
| Deferred cost | −1 victory point at the end of the game for each huarango felled (both free and subsequent) |
:::

Remove a huarango token adjacent to one of your connected pegs. Gain 2 water tokens immediately and flip one valley field tile without spending an action.

> [!WARNING]
> THE DOUBLE DILEMMA: (1) When to use it? Early (more water available, higher El Niño risk for all) or late (less risk, but water arrives too late)? (2) Is −1 pt worth the +2 water + free field? With all 4 players potentially felling, the counter may cross the El Niño threshold — another −3 pt for everyone.
