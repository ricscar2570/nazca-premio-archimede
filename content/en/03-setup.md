# PART III — SETUP (5–7 minutes)

## Step 1 — Set up the pampa board

Place the 4 assembled Quercetti panels in the centre of the table. Overlay the pampa overlay, aligning it to the edges. Leave clear space on all 4 sides — players' fields will go there.

## Step 2 — Choose clans and materials

Players choose or draw clans. Each player takes: clan peg pouch, cord reel, clan screen, clan summary card.

The Orca also takes the neutral peg pouch (black/transparent). The shared puquio pouch (terracotta) goes in the centre of the table.

## Step 3 — Huarango

Place the 6 huarango tokens at positions H1–H6 on the overlay. Everyone can see them.

## Step 4 — El Niño

Shuffle the 3 El Niño cards (thresholds 4, 5, 6). Draw one face-down and place it beside the board. No one looks at it.

## Step 5 — Secret geoglyph cards

Shuffle the 24 monumental geoglyph cards. Deal one face-down to each player. The card shows the secret path between sources. It is never revealed until the Revelation.

## Step 6 — Geometric objectives

Reveal 3 geometric objective cards. Place them face-up beside the board — they are open objectives for all players.

## Step 7 — Choose a slope

Clockwise from the youngest player, each player chooses their slope: North, South, East or West — that is, which edge of the board corresponds to their valley.

> [!NOTE]
> The slope is NOT fixed — two clans may choose the same side. The choice is strategic: consider where the sources of your geoglyph path are, how far the edge is, and where opponents are heading.

| Slope | Board edge | Nearby sources | Tactical note |
|-------|------------|----------------|---------------|
| North | Row 1 (top) | S1, S2, S3 | Good for paths starting at the top. Hummingbird and Condor bases. |
| South | Row 27 (bottom) | S9, S10, S11, S12 | Good for dense paths in the lower quadrant. Spider and Orca bases. |
| East | Column T (right) | S2, S5, S7, S10, S12 | Access to right-side sources. Risk: Condor starts here. |
| West | Column A (left) | S1, S4, S6, S9, S11 | Access to left-side sources. Risk: Spider starts here. |

## Step 8 — Place valley field tiles

Each player takes their 5 field tiles (2 alluvial + 2 clay + 1 sandy) and places them on the table along their chosen slope — touching the board edge, soil side up.

The layout is free. This initial choice is already tactical: tiles closer to the centre of the edge will be reachable sooner.

## Step 9 — Starting water and settlement

Each player takes 2 water tokens from the supply. Then clockwise, each player plants their first peg at their clan's base node (board edge) and stretches the first cord segment.

The Condor's spying power activates during rounds, not in setup.

## Step 10 — Start

Place the round token on space 1. The youngest player goes first. Clockwise order.
