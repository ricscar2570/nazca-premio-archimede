# PART VIII — THE VALLEY

## 8.1  Placing field tiles

Each player places their 5 field tiles on the table touching the board edge on their chosen slope. The layout is free — the player chooses order and position.

Tiles start with the soil side up (brown/dry). An irrigated tile shows the green side. A tile with a crop shows the crop tile on top.

> [!NOTE]
> FREE PLACEMENT AND ORCA POWER: Free field tile placement does not interact with the Orca's CANAL bonus. The Orca's CANAL bonus is calculated entirely on the board (nodes adjacent to the border node touched) — it does not depend on where the field tiles physically are on the table. There is no need to align tiles to specific grid coordinates.

## 8.2  How to irrigate a field

- Your chain from the puquio must touch the board edge on your chosen slope (peg in the last row/column — see Part V.3).
- Spend 1 action (IRRIGATE TO VALLEY).
- Flip 1 field tile to the irrigated side. Water cost: 0.
- The field remains irrigated for the whole game — it never dries out.

**Why 0 water:** the real cost was already paid in the actions and cord used to build the chain from the source to the edge.

## 8.3  The agricultural cycle in the valley

| Step | Where | Action | Cost |
|------|-------|--------|------|
| 1. Irrigation | Valley — off board | Flip 1 field tile to irrigated side | 1 action · 0 water |
| 2. Sowing | Valley — irrigated field | Place crop tile on seed side | 1 action · 1 water (maize/beans/squash) · 2 water (cotton) · 0 on clay (beans) |
| 3. Maturation | Automatic — phase 1 | Advances 1 step each round. On ideal terrain matures 1 round earlier. | 0 actions · 0 water |
| 4. Harvest | Valley — mature field | Take tile, add to scoring area. Beans: +1 water. Cotton on sandy: +1 water. | 1 action · 0 water |
| 5. Re-sowing | Valley — free irrigated field | Field stays irrigated — sow immediately | 1 action · 1 water (maize/beans/squash) · 2 water (cotton) · 0 on clay (beans) |

## 8.4  The Three Sisters — Set Collection

The Andean agricultural triad: maize + beans + squash. If at game end your scoring area contains at least 1 mature maize, 1 mature beans and 1 mature squash, you earn the Three Sisters bonus.

:::twocol
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Condition | ≥1 maize + ≥1 beans + ≥1 squash mature in scoring area |
| Base bonus | +5 pt per completed triad |
| Adjacency required? | NO — pure set collection. Fields may be anywhere along the slope. |
| Repeatability | The triad can be completed multiple times with enough fields |
:::

## 8.5  Huarango felling and the valley

When you fell a Huarango (free action 1×/game), in addition to the +2 water you may immediately flip 1 valley field tile without spending the IRRIGATE TO VALLEY action.

This makes the felling moment particularly powerful: +2 water + 1 free irrigated field + 1 saved action = a massively boosted turn.
